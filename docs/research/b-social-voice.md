## 4. Visual identity in the wild

- **Logo:** crimson disc or rounded square with a white "LS" monogram; the lower bowl of the S holds four piano keys. Wordmark "LYRA" bold + "STYLE" light, uppercase sans, with "MUZIČKI INSTRUMENTI" or "MUSICAL INSTRUMENTS". Used on black or white.
- **Colours actually used:** crimson, black, white. Real-world accents: pine, sunburst guitar finishes, Markbass yellow.
- **Off-brand strays to ignore:** neon pink-blue "LYRA STYLE" frame (2025), green "AKCIJA" poster, AI lyre signage, AI flat illustrations.
- **Recurring real motifs:** the wall of guitars shot down its length; the Martin pine wall; the bench with a guitar mid-restring; strings fanning from a headstock; the red floor and LED ticker of the old shop.
- **Photography:** mostly manufacturer press shots or reposts. Their own pictures are phone snapshots under shop lighting — honest, unstyled.

## 5. Brand voice as it really is

Based on Google replies, the Google owner update, Facebook, YouTube titles and the few genuine posts.

1. **Familijaran** — greets reviewers by first name or nickname; signs off with a wink.
2. **Iskren** — admits the builders ran late and that the location is not ideal.
3. **Muzičarski** — talks about tone, practice and players; admires skill ("kad neko zna").
4. **Šeretski** — running joke of "we stock everything except the artist in the video".
5. **Praktičan** — stock, price, model, phone. No lifestyle language.

- **Typical fragments:** "na lageru", "kod nas možete naći", "skoro pa sve", "za svakoga ponešto", "kad neko zna", "trudimo se", "samo jako", "lep vikend svima".
- **Address:** "ti" by default with players, in job ads and in most replies; "Vi" for formal replies to strangers; plural "vi" in offers.
- **Script and style:** Latin, usually typed without č/š/ž, with typos; English dropped in freely. Cyrillic only on holiday graphics. One-line captions, 1–3 emoji (🦾 🙏 ;) 🎵), 2–5 brand hashtags plus #lyrastylebeograd.
- **Proud of:** breadth ("everything for every instrument"), the 500+ guitars, Martin, wind consumables, the drum department, staff who play, famous musicians being old friends, ties to Guča and music schools.

## 6. Implications for the new site's tone

1. **Write as the owner talks, cleaned up.** Short, first-name-warm, with correct diacritics and no typos. Latin primary; a Cyrillic toggle is optional.
2. **"Ti" for players, "Vi" for parents and schools.** Hero and product copy in "ti"; buying guides for a child's first instrument in "Vi".
3. **Lead with people and the bench.** Real photos of the owner, Vedran and the team earn the engagement the feed otherwise lacks. Commission a shoot; use nothing AI-made.
4. **Turn the weak spot into a feature.** The shop is hard to find. Make "how to find us" a designed moment: atrium entrance, Smolućska 16V, parking, a short walk-in video.
5. **Claim depth, with numbers.** 500+ guitars, 400 m², 1,074 Google reviews at 4.8, trying before buying, the reed-and-string wall. Keep the dry humour in microcopy, never in facts.
6. **English secondary = plain and brief.** Many reviewers are expats praising hard-to-find strings and helpful staff. Give English visitors hours, directions, "try before you buy" and contact — not a translated essay.

**Gaps to fill with the owner:** founding year; street number confirmation; staff names and roles; whether repair and set-up is a formal service; which brands they officially distribute.
