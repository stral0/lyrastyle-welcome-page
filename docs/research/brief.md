# Lyra Style — brief and concept directions (synthesis of research a–e)

## Brief
**Who they are.** Lyra Style d.o.o., musical instruments, Novi Beograd. Owner-run (Dragan Jagodić answers nearly every Google review himself), in business since 1989 per a 2018 Novosti feature (not stated on their own site — confirm). 7,933 products, 347 brands, eight departments. Moved in April 2026 to a 400 m² shop at Partizanske avijacije 16 (LUX 51, lamela 2; on foot via Smolućska 16V, atrium). Google: 4.8★ from 1,074 reviews, the largest review base of any instrument shop in Novi Beograd.
**What people love.** Staff who play and advise; being allowed to try instruments; hard-to-find consumables. **What they complain about.** The new shop is hard to find; the site says nothing about who they are ("O nama" is Lorem ipsum; every page is noindex).
**References worth showing.** Steinway D-274 for the Belgrade City Assembly, Yamaha C7 for Jugokoncert, the Philharmonic, the National Theatre, the Army orchestra, embassy AV systems, the Slavija fountain sound system, awards at Guča.
**Competition.** Six rivals within 3.5 km (Player, Artist, Rock Corner, Music Box, Guitar Salon, Acoustic Union) plus Mitros and Thomann. All of them sell on range, price and instalments. None lets you hear anything online, none tells a story, none is a place.
**Buyers.** Parents of music-school pupils (June–September), wedding and kafana musicians (arranger keyboards, PA), bedroom producers, weekend rockers, institutions (schools, theatres, churches needing speech/choir reinforcement).
**Job of the site.** A brand layer over the WooCommerce shop: make a first-time visitor feel what the shop is like (you can hear it before you own it), then hand them to the shop or bring them through the door. Serbian first, English toggle.
**Voice.** Musician to musician: plain, candid, a little dry, "ti". Facts over superlatives.
**Constraints.** One self-contained HTML file, no assets, no external requests, procedural visuals and sound; keyboard, reduced-motion and mobile support; architecture of the reference artifact (scenes → beats, state-derived timeline, in-canvas type, hand-written post, bespoke transitions).

## Direction A — "Prvo čuj" (Listen first)  ← PICKED
Sound made visible and playable. Each scene is one physical truth about sound that the visitor can touch: a string you pull and release (→ the seven strings of a lyre → inside a concert grand), sand on a struck plate drawing Chladni figures, a pressure wave crossing Novi Beograd in real time (11 seconds to Sava Centar), then the sky: Lyra the constellation, its eight stars the eight departments, and an invitation to come and hear it for real.
*Why it wins:* it is the shop's actual advantage (try before you buy) turned into an experience no competitor has; it needs no photography; physics rendered as light is the kind of imagery that can be made stunning procedurally; the name gives both the lyre and the constellation for free.
*Risk:* abstract if the copy does not keep it grounded in real facts — so every beat carries one.

## Direction B — "Grad koji svira" (The city that plays)
Novi Beograd's blocks at night as an instrument: facades are step sequencers, windows light up as notes, each block hosts one department (a drummer's garage in Blok 45, a piano in a living room, a PA on a rooftop). The visitor composes a loop across the skyline and the shop is where it all comes from.
*Strength:* fiercely local, playful, shareable. *Weakness:* leans on architectural modelling and character scenes that are hard to make beautiful without assets; says less about the shop itself.

## Direction C — "Jedan život u muzici" (One life in music)
One musician's life in five rooms: the first small violin, the music-school corridor, the wedding stage, the studio, the concert hall with a Steinway — each a lit diorama, each mapped to a persona from the research and a department.
*Strength:* speaks directly to parents and working musicians, emotionally legible. *Weakness:* five figurative interiors is the largest modelling load of the three; with no photography it risks looking like stock 3D.

## Decision
The concept gate could not be held (owner of the decision asleep, explicit instruction to proceed without questions). Picked A; B's city survives as A's third scene, C's "first violin to concert grand" as A's first.
