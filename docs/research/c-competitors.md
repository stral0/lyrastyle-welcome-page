## 2. Sea of sameness, and the empty ground

What they say (pages read):

| Claim type | Who uses it |
|---|---|
| Biggest range | Lyra itself: "Najveća ponuda", "Najveća selekcija u Srbiji" [S3] |
| Price and credit | Player (12 rata bez kamate, lowest-price guarantee) [S5]; Sky ("uštedi na ceni", 12 rata) [S9] |
| Leadership | Player: "jedan od lidera" [S6] |
| Shop plus workshop | Rock Corner [S8]; Acoustic Union [S11] |
| Nothing at all | Mitros: no tagline, no about text on the home page [S12] |

Lyra's current headline is the weakest version of the first row: Mitros lists nearly a thousand Yamaha items and three cities [S12], Player 70+ staff [S6]. "Najveća" is not believable and not ownable.

What none of them says or does (on the pages I could read; Artist and Guitar Salon unread):

1. **No sound.** Every site is a silent grid of thumbnails and RSD prices [S5][S8][S9][S11][S12]. Nobody lets you hear an instrument.
2. **No people.** No founder, no named staff, no story. Player's about page is corporate [S6]; Lyra's is placeholder [S4]. Yet Google reviewers thank Mitros staff by first name [G9].
3. **No place.** Nobody frames the shop as a room worth travelling to. Lyra's atrium location is called hard to find [G1]; unaddressed, that is a flaw, and addressed, it is a hidden-room story.
4. **No answer to Thomann.** None explains why buying here beats ordering from Germany.
5. **No beginner or parent narrative** beyond blog posts.
6. **No immersive web presence.** All are template catalogues; a WebGL narrative has no local precedent in this set.

## 3. International bar

| Brand | Why it is loved beyond price | Borrowable by a small shop |
|---|---|---|
| **Sweetwater** | A named sales engineer per customer; 154 of them, new hires trained 13 weeks before taking a call [S14][S15]. Candy in every box [S16]. 55-point guitar inspection, free 2-year warranty [S17] | One named person per customer; a published pre-sale checklist; a small signature object in every bag |
| **Thomann** | Family-owned since 1954, 1,660 staff, 30-day money-back since 1995, 3-year warranty since 2005 [S18]; over 9 million customers, 19 languages (2019 figures) [S19] | Guarantees stated as plain promises with dates attached |
| **Andertons** | One family shop in Guildford since 1964, turned into a global name by YouTube; over 1 million subscribers (Sept 2025) [S20][S21]. Lee Anderton says rivals treated the internet as a fad [S22] | Personality on camera; one location is enough |
| **Reverb** | Marketplace known for vintage and second-hand gear; leadership speaks of a "music-making community"; independent again after the 2025 sale by Etsy [S23][S24] | Used gear and trade-in as community, not clearance |

**Thomann and Serbia (non-EU):**
- Thomann removes German VAT on non-EU orders [S25]; the recipient then pays local VAT plus import and customs duties on arrival, and Thomann does not issue EUR.1 origin forms [S26].
- Serbian side, per a 2024 press summary citing the Customs Administration: purchases from companies above EUR 50 attract duty and 20% VAT; VAT is charged on value plus duty, and shipping cost is added to the customs base [S27]. The exact duty rate for instruments sent by a company: **unverified**.
- Thomann's shipping fee and transit time for Serbia sit behind a country selector I could not read [S28]: **unverified**. Serbian forum threads on Thomann orders exist but were unreadable.
- Serbia was not among the eight countries given cheaper Thomann shipping in October 2025 [S29].
- That many Serbian musicians order from Thomann is the brief's premise; I found no figure: **unverified**.

**What Thomann structurally cannot offer:** playing the instrument today; no customs paperwork or surprise charge at the door; a counter to return to for warranty or setup; conversation in Serbian with someone who remembers you. Local installments and in-house servis are obvious additions, but Lyra's site states neither [S3]; **confirm with client before claiming**.

## 5. Three positions Lyra Style could own

1. **"The one room in Novi Beograd where you hear the instrument before you own it."** Evidence: Lyra's reviewers already single out trying instruments [G1]; every rival site is silent [S5][S9][S12]; Thomann cannot offer it at any price. It also converts the hard-to-find atrium [G1][S1] into a destination, and it is the natural spine for a sound-led WebGL narrative [S30][S31].

2. **"A shop with names, not a catalogue with a cart."** Evidence: the most-loved retailer in the category is built on a personal sales engineer [S14][S15]; Belgrade reviewers name individual staff while the shops' own sites name nobody [G9][S6][S12]. Precondition: Lyra's about page is currently Lorem ipsum [S4]; the real story must be gathered from the client.

3. **"Yours today, looked after here: no customs, no waiting, no guessing."** Evidence: Thomann strips German VAT but leaves the Serbian buyer to settle VAT and duties and gives no origin certificate [S25][S26][S27]; nearby rivals ask customers to phone ahead for stock [S12][G5]. Precondition: same-day stock, servis and installments must be confirmed as real Lyra services [S3].

Retire "Najveća selekcija u Srbiji": it is the category's default claim and Lyra cannot win it against Mitros or Player [S6][S12].
