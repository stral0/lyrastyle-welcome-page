# D — Who buys instruments and audio gear in Serbia

Research date: 26 Nov 2026. Web sources only; "est." = my estimate, not a sourced figure. FX: 1 EUR = 117.61 RSD (NBS, 17 Nov 2026 — https://www.novosti.rs/ekonomija/vesti/1671862/zvanicni-srednji-kurs-dinara-17-novembar-2026-evro-dolar).

## 1. Music education

- **Scale.** Sources disagree: AEC counts 72 elementary + secondary music schools with 20,000+ pupils, mostly state-run and free (2016 — https://aec-music.eu/members/national-overviews/serbia/); Wikipedia says "more than 100" (70 primary, 30 secondary) (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Music_schools_in_Serbia). Working number: ~70–100 schools, 20k+ pupils (est. range). One Belgrade school alone (Davorin Jenko, Rakovica) reports 900+ pupils (https://muzicar.rs/muzicke-skole-u-srbiji/).
- **Structure.** Primary music school runs parallel to regular school: 6-year track (violin, cello, guitar, piano, harmonika, flute…), 4-year track, 2-year singing; every school has an entrance exam testing ear, pitch, rhythm (AEC, above). Entry age cap is 9 for violin/piano/guitar/harmonika and 11 for clarinet, sax, trumpet, percussion (https://www.isidorbajic.edu.rs/skolovanje/osnovna-muzicka-skola/).
- **Season.** Entrance exams late May–early June, classes from 1 September (https://rtvparacin.rs/2025/09/05/pocetak-nove-skolske-godine-u-muzickoj-skoli-milenko-zivkovic/); Isidor Bajić enrols 11–12 June (link above). So the first-instrument purchase window is **mid-June to mid-September**; New Year gifts and birthdays are a second peak (est.).
- **Demand by instrument.** A school director says guitar, violin and piano draw the most applicants every year (rtvparacin link). Harmonika is a full 6-year school instrument and has its own national contest, "Prva harmonika Srbije" in Sokobanja, running since 1962 with 100+ finalists (https://www.novosti.rs/turizam/vesti/1632887/prva-harmonika-srbije-sokobanja-festival-koji-smete-propustite).
- **Parents co-fund school instruments** via "voluntary donations" paid in nine monthly instalments (Isidor Bajić link) — they already think in rates.
- **Higher ed.** Five state institutions, ~1,500 music students (AEC). FMU Beograd: founded 1937, nearly 1,000 students, 200+ teachers (https://www.fmu.bg.ac.rs/about-us/). Akademija umetnosti Novi Sad has a Department of Music (https://en.akademija.uns.ac.rs/music/).
- **Private schools** exist alongside tutors (AEC); rock/pop schools such as Master Blaster teach guitar, keys, singing, drums (https://www.mirandre.com/muzicke-skole/beograd). No market-size data found.
- **What parents buy (Lyra's own prices):** Gewa 3/4 violin set 19,900 RSD (https://lyrastyle.rs/category); Yamaha YDP-145 digital piano 129,990 RSD (https://lyrastyle.rs/product/yamaha-arius-ydp145r-elektricni-klavir). Fractional violins get outgrown — trade-up/buy-back is an unmet hook (est.).

## 2. Churches and choirs

- **Demography.** 2022 census: Orthodox 5,387,426 (81.1%), Catholic 257,269 (3.8%), Protestant 54,678 (0.8%) (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Religion_in_Serbia).
- **Orthodox = voice only.** Instruments are not permitted in the liturgy; the Karlovac chant shaped by Mokranjac dominates, Byzantine chant with ison survives in monasteries like Kovilj (https://vreme.com/kultura/eho-nebeske-pesme). Do not show organs or bands in an Orthodox context.
- **What Orthodox parishes buy: speech and choir reinforcement.** Real installs exist: an audio system designed for Sv. Marko, Belgrade (http://telit.etf.rs/audio-sistem-za-hram-svetog-marka-u-beogradu/) and for the Saborna crkva, Novi Sad (https://www.avlprojekt.rs/projekat/110441/ozvucenje-za-sabornu-crkvu-u-novom-sadu). A Serbian integrator's typical spec: slim line-array speakers, a wireless lavalier for the priest, 2–4 choir mics, DSP mixer with feedback suppression, USB interface for streaming; the problem to solve is 4–8 s reverberation in stone, domed spaces (https://ozvucenjeprostora.rs/resenja/crkve/). Note it specifies Audac, Sennheiser, Shure, AKG — all brands Lyra lists (https://lyrastyle.rs/).
- **Also (est., unsourced):** battery PA for litije, slava and parish-hall events; tuning forks/pitch pipes and folders for choir conductors; recorders for choir archives.
- **Catholic/Protestant (Vojvodina, Belgrade).** Catholics cluster in northern Vojvodina (Hungarians, Croats); Protestants around Bački Petrovac, Kovačica and Novi Sad, led by the Slovak Lutheran church, with Adventists, Baptists and Pentecostals about a fifth (Religion in Serbia link). Vojvodina has 173 pipe organs, mostly under-used, and churches do not train organists (https://www.021.rs/info/vojvodina/213335/orgulje-u-vojvodini-kako-sacuvati-drevni-zvuk) → digital pianos/organs and keyboards as substitutes; evangelical worship bands need full backline + PA (est.). Small but loyal niche.

## 3. Working musicians

- **Weddings are the engine.** A band now costs about 3,000–4,000 EUR per wedding, up from ~1,500; total wedding 20,000–25,000 EUR for ~200 guests; autumn is traditional peak (https://biznis.kurir.rs/novcanik/10135384/koliko-kosta-svadba-u-srbiji-glavni-troskovi). Gear is a business investment paid back in a few gigs.
- **Arranger keyboards.** Flagships sit at 4–5 average net salaries: Korg Pa5X-61 406,900 RSD (https://mitrosmusic.com/proizvod/25263-Korg-PA5X-61), Yamaha Genos 2 507,490 RSD discounted from 634,363 (https://www.mitrosmusic.com/proizvod/27921-Yamaha-Genos-2). Pa5X ships with quarter-tone/Arabic scale controls (Mitros link) — relevant for folk/oriental ornamentation. Ketron: no Serbian data found.
- **Trubači.** Guča, founded 1961, draws an estimated 150,000 visitors a day; about 1,500 performers in 2006 (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gu%C4%8Da_Trumpet_Festival). Lyra has sponsored orchestras there (https://lyrastyle.rs/blog).
- **Harmonika / tamburaši.** Accordion is the wedding-and-kafana instrument (Sokobanja link); tamburica is a Vojvodina tradition with its own Novi Sad festival (https://www.novosti.rs/scena/poznati/1281570/publika-uzivala-umecu-majstora-pera-zice-novom-sadu-danas-zavrsen-16-svetski-tamburica-fest) and is taught in music schools (Isidor Bajić link).
- **Rock/metal/indie.** Bands rent rooms by the hour: Studio Playground has four rooms from ~1,350 RSD/h (https://studioplayground.rs/rooms/). These players have day jobs; they buy guitars, pedals, cymbals, not backline (est.).
- **Clubs/DJ.** Belgrade's scene grew from 1990s wartime raves; Drugstore (2012, ex-slaughterhouse) is the techno reference; local DJs are underpaid versus guests (https://www.electronicbeats.net/how-belgrades-club-scene-grew-from-wartime-rave-roots). **EXIT** left Serbia after 2025, toured abroad in 2026, and returns to Petrovaradin in July 2027 (https://n1info.rs/english/news/exit-festival-to-return-home/) — a live "homecoming" mood.
- **Jazz:** no numbers found; secondary schools do have jazz departments (AEC).

## 4. Studios, creators, B2B

- **Podcasters/streamers.** Academic work confirms podcasting is increasingly popular in Serbia, without market figures (https://ideas.repec.org/a/cog/meanco/v13y2025a9050.html). Lyra's blog has a "first microphone" post but nothing for studio/creator buyers (https://lyrastyle.rs/blog) — a content gap. Reference prices: Yamaha HS7 37,550 RSD each (https://lyrastyle.rs/product/yamaha-hs7-aktivni-studijski-monitor); Behringer X32 349,804 RSD (https://lyrastyle.rs/product/behringer-x32).
- **Institutions buy big, via tender.** Novi Sad bought 86 pianos worth 280,000 EUR for its new music and ballet school (https://www.novosti.rs/kultura/vesti/999330/grad-novi-sad-kupio-86-novih-muzickih-instrumenata-klaviri-potpisom-marte-argeric). **Lyra itself** won a public tender to equip the Serbian Army orchestra (250,000 EUR) and in 2005 delivered 20 pianos — Steinway, Yamaha, digital — to the City Assembly, Terazije Theatre and JDP (sponsored article, 2018 — https://www.novosti.rs/vesti/kultura.71.html:760888-Lyra-Style--muzicka-magija-koja-traje).
- **Grassroots.** A primary school in Aleksinac built a rock club from a drum kit bought by the principal plus donated guitar and amp (https://n1info.rs/vesti/direktorka-kupila-bubanj-bajaga-i-instruktori-gitaru-i-pojacalo-za-skolski-rok-bend/).
- Hotels, event/rental firms, houses of culture: no figures found; they need quotes, invoices by bank transfer and installation advice (est.).

## 5. Economics

- **Income.** Average net salary Aug 2026: 117,282 RSD (~997 EUR); median 93,325 RSD (~793 EUR); +8.7% real y/y (https://biznis.rs/vesti/srbija/prosecna-zarada-u-srbiji-u-avgustu-iznosila-117-282-dinara/). Design for the median: a 130k digital piano is 1.4 monthly median wages; a Genos is 5.4.
- **Instalments are the norm.** Cheques still matter — 5.4 million realised, 25.7 bn RSD, Jan–Sep 2024 — but interest-free card instalments grow faster (+19% count, +31% value) (https://biznis.rs/vesti/srbija/rast-broja-realizovanih-cekova-na-domacem-trzistu-u-2024-godini/). Competitor Player.rs shows a 2–12 instalment picker (Banca Intesa credit cards), a lower cash price and free delivery over 15,000 RSD on the product page (https://www.player.rs/dj/dj-gramofoni/technics-sl-1200mk7). **Lyra's terms list only cards, cash on delivery and bank transfer — no rates or cheques online** (https://lyrastyle.rs/terms). Biggest commercial gap found.
- **Thomann.** Non-EU orders are not charged German VAT (https://www.thomannmusic.com/faq_question_do_i_have_to_pay_value_added_tax_vat.html). On arrival in Serbia, parcels over 50 EUR pay 20% VAT + 10% duty + inspection fee; under 50 EUR, VAT only (Pošta director, Mar 2026 — https://biznis.kurir.rs/info-biz/9944885/kako-izbeci-carinu-na-pakete-iznad-50-evra). EU-origin goods can be duty-free with proof of origin; VAT still applies (https://biznis.rs/preduzetnik/porezi/sta-se-sve-moze-uneti-u-srbiju-bez-carine/). Est. arithmetic: German shelf price ÷1.19 ×1.10 ×1.20 ≈ **1.11× the Thomann sticker**, plus shipping and clearance, minus easy returns. So Thomann is rarely cheaper for one item; its pull is range and Harley Benton-type house brands, which resurface on KupujemProdajem (https://www.kupujemprodajem.com/muzika-i-instrumenti/gitare-elektricne/harley-benton-te-20bk-standard-series-bundle/oglas/164369536). Forum threads on the topic exist (https://yu-midi.org/forum/showthread.php?t=31048) but could not be read.
- **Second-hand.** KupujemProdajem: 3M+ registered users, 5.5M+ listings (2023 — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/KupujemProdajem). Lyra already runs a "Kupujem Prodajem" listing page and a B-stock category (https://lyrastyle.rs/blog, https://lyrastyle.rs/).
- **Trust signals.** Lyra shows "Povremeno na lageru" on big-ticket items and states warranty inconsistently (X32, HS7, YDP links). 14-day withdrawal, 2–5 day delivery, free shipping threshold stated two ways (200 EUR vs 25,000 RSD) (https://lyrastyle.rs/terms). Buyers expect a stamped warranty and a local service address (est.).

## 6. Cultural hooks

- **Lyra Style.** Founded **1989**; owner Dragan Jagodić; Steinway & Sons and Yamaha pianos; 12 years sponsoring Guitar Art Festival; 130+ guitars supplied through a US Embassy donation; YU Grupa guitarist Petar Jelić is a customer and blog author (2018 sponsored article, above). Shop: Partizanske avijacije 6, Novi Beograd, Mon–Fri 10–19, Sat 10–15; zero public reviews on PlanPlus (https://www.planplus.rs/lyra-style-muzicki-instrumenti/93120). "O nama" is still lorem ipsum (https://lyrastyle.rs/o-nama). No forum reputation found, good or bad. 7,933 products online (https://lyrastyle.rs/category). Current tagline: "Najveća selekcija u Srbiji".
- **Novi Beograd.** Sava Centar (Blok 19, architect Stojan Maksimović, opened 1977–79, 4,000-seat Plava dvorana) hosted Miles Davis, Nina Simone, Ray Charles, B.B. King, Lou Reed; reconstruction by Delta from 2021 (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sava_Centar). Blokovi: ~79,000 residents; Blocks 45/70 "Naselje Sunca"; Riblja Čorba's "Neću da živim u Bloku 65" (1982) (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blokovi).
- **Yu rock.** "Paket aranžman" (Jugoton, Feb 1981: Idoli, Električni orgazam, Šarlo akrobata) is called the zero point of Yugoslav rock (https://n1info.rs/magazin/showbiz/popovic-o-paket-aranzmanu/). SKC and Dom omladine as its stages: common knowledge, not verified here.
- **The lyre.** Invented by Hermes, given to Apollo, taught to **Orpheus — a Thracian, i.e. a Balkan musician**; seven strings; Plato's instrument for education (https://www.worldhistory.org/Lyre). Seven strings ↔ Lyra's eight departments minus accessories: a ready-made structural metaphor (my idea).
- **Sayings** (common knowledge, verify wording): "Ko peva, zlo ne misli"; Zmaj's "Pesma nas je održala, njojzi hvala". Sokobanja organisers: "Harmonika je pesma u našim srcima" (Novosti link, §1).

### Six implications for narrative and tone

1. **Lead with 1989 and proof, not "largest selection".** 37 years, Steinway, the Army orchestra, theatres, Guitar Art are rarer assets than SKU count — and currently invisible ("O nama" is lorem ipsum).
2. **Tell one musical life in chapters:** first instrument → school → stage → studio → institution. It maps onto all five personas and onto the site's existing line "gde god da se nalaze na svom muzičkom putovanju".
3. **Two registers of Serbian.** Warm, reassuring "Vi" for parents and institutions; direct "ti" for players and producers. Short sentences, no English jargon where a Serbian word exists (ozvučenje, klavijature, svirka, proba). English toggle is for expats and partners, not the emotional core.
4. **Honour both Serbias of sound.** Truba, harmonika, tambura and the arranger keyboard pay as many bills as the Telecaster. A WebGL experience that only quotes rock will miss the wedding economy; one that only quotes folk will lose Luka and Marko. Orpheus the Thracian and the lyre bridge both without kitsch. Keep church imagery vocal and acoustic.
5. **Answer Thomann and KP on the page.** Try it here, landed price with PDV, warranty, service, rates. Surface instalments, stock status and delivery promise in the experience itself — today they are missing or contradictory.
6. **Root it in Novi Beograd.** Concrete, light, river, Sava Centar's modernism, the Blokovi — a visual language no competitor owns. Time campaigns to the real calendar: June enrolment, September, December gifts, spring–autumn wedding season, EXIT's July 2027 return.
